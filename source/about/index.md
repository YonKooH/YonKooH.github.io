## whoami

I'm **Oussema Toujeni**, known online as **YonKoo**, a SOC Analyst based in Tunisia.

At work I monitor client environments with **Microsoft Sentinel**, **Elastic XDR**, **Defender** and **Trend Micro**. I triage alerts by criticality, investigate the true positives, notify clients, and write vulnerability and threat-intel bulletins.
